    02 — About

    A designer who goes inside the work

    I'm a Senior Product Designer. For four years I was at Pixis, a marketing-technology company, where I was promoted twice in two years.

    I design for experts: people who need their tools to be right, clear and fast, and to fit how they already work. That has held true across analytics, automation, billing and AI. So I shadow real working sessions before I design, and I hand over systems, not just screens.
